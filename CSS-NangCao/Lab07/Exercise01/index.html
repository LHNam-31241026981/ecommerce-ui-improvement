Document flow

A wonderful serenity has taken possession of my entire soul, like these sweet mornings of spring which I enjoy with my whole heart. I am alone, and feel the charm of existence in this spot, which was created for the bliss of souls like mine. I am so happy, my dear friend, so absorbed in the exquisite sense of mere tranquil existence, that I neglect my talents.

Reviews

When the lovely valley teems with vapour around me, and the meridian sun strikes the upper surface of the foliage, I throw myself down among the tall grass by the trickling stream. As I lie close to the earth, a thousand unknown plants are noticed by me; when I hear the little world among the stalks and grow familiar with insects and flies, I feel the presence of the natural world in an eternity of bliss.
